What to eat in pregnancy and foods to avoid

You do not need to eat for two. Mia explains what helps you and your baby, and which foods to avoid, without the stress.

Start free
01

Eat a varied diet

Fruit, vegetables, wholegrains, protein and dairy or alternatives. You need only a little extra energy in the last trimester.

02

Key supplements

In the UK, folic acid until week 12 and vitamin D all pregnancy are advised. Ask your midwife what suits you.

03

Foods to avoid

Avoid raw or undercooked eggs not marked Lion, unpasteurised soft cheeses, pâté, liver and raw or undercooked meat. Limit some fish.

04

Caffeine and alcohol

Keep caffeine under 200 mg a day, about two mugs of instant coffee. The safest choice with alcohol is none.

Based on NHS guidance on foods to avoid and vitamins in pregnancy. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Do I need to eat for two?

No. You need about 200 extra calories a day only in the last three months.

Is it safe to eat fish?

Yes, and one or two portions a week is good. Avoid shark, swordfish and marlin, and limit oily fish.
