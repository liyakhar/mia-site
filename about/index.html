About Mia.

Mia is a pregnancy and natural birth companion for iPhone. She gives you short lessons, plain answers and a few simple tools, from your first scan to the first weeks at home.

Who it is for

Pregnant people and their partners who want to feel ready, especially for a natural birth.

Mia is not a doctor

Mia teaches and supports. Your midwife and doctor look after you, so call them if something feels wrong.

Your privacy

Your records stay on your iPhone. No ads, ever.
