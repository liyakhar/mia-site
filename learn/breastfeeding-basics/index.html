Breastfeeding basics for new parents

Feeding is a skill you and your baby learn together. Here are the basics, and where to find help, whatever you choose.

Start free
01

The first feed

Skin-to-skin soon after birth helps baby find the breast. Ask for help with the first feed.

02

A good latch

Baby’s mouth wide open, chin touching the breast, and feeding should not hurt after the first few seconds.

03

How often

Newborns feed often, about 8 to 12 times in 24 hours. Feed when baby shows early hunger signs.

04

Ask for help

Pain, a baby who is not feeding or not having wet nappies need a call to your midwife or feeding specialist.

Based on WHO and NHS guidance on breastfeeding and the UNICEF Baby Friendly Initiative. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

What if I choose to bottle feed?

That is a valid choice. Mia explains bottle feeding safely too.

Is it normal for feeding to hurt at first?

A little tenderness can be common. Ongoing pain is not, so ask for help with the latch.
