Pregnancy week by week

Each week of pregnancy brings small changes for you and your baby. Mia shows one calm view of your week: how big your baby is, what your body is doing and one thing worth doing now.

Weeks 1 to 13

The first trimester. Tiredness and nausea are common. Your first scan is usually around week 12.

Weeks 14 to 27

The second trimester. Many people feel more energy. You may feel the first movements.

Weeks 28 to 40

The third trimester. Baby grows fast. From week 24, notice your baby’s usual pattern of movements.

After 40 weeks

Many babies arrive after their due date. Your midwife will talk with you about your options.

Based on NHS pregnancy guidance and WHO antenatal care recommendations. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

When should I call my midwife?

If your baby moves less than usual, if you bleed, or if something feels wrong. Do not wait for your next visit.

Does Mia replace my check-ups?

No. Mia helps you understand your week. Your midwife and doctor look after you.
