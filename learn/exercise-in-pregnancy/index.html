Exercise in pregnancy: what is safe

Moving in pregnancy is good for you and your baby. Mia shows what is safe, what to avoid and when to stop.

Start free
01

Keep moving

About 150 minutes of moderate activity a week is advised if you are well. Walking and swimming are easy places to start.

02

Strength and stretching

Gentle strength work, yoga and pelvic floor exercises are fine for most people. Tell the teacher you are pregnant.

03

What to avoid

Avoid contact sports, activities with a risk of falling, and lying flat on your back for long periods later on.

04

Stop and call if

You feel dizzy, short of breath, have chest pain, bleeding, fluid leaking or less baby movement.

Based on NHS and UK Chief Medical Officers’ guidance on exercise in pregnancy. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Can I keep running?

Many people can if they were runners before. Listen to your body and talk to your midwife.

Is exercise bad for the baby?

No. For most healthy pregnancies, regular activity is safe and linked with benefits.
