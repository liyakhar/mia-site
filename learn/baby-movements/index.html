Baby movements in pregnancy

Your baby’s movements tell you they are well. Mia helps you learn your baby’s usual pattern and what to do if it changes.

When you feel them

Many people feel the first movements between weeks 16 and 24. It may be later in a first pregnancy.

Know your baby’s pattern

There is no set number of kicks. Get to know what is usual for your baby.

Less movement

If your baby moves less than usual, call your midwife or maternity unit the same day. Do not wait until the next day.

Do not use apps to reassure you

Do not rely on a home doppler or an app to check on baby. Always call your midwife.

Based on NHS guidance on baby movements and Tommy’s and RCOG advice on reduced movements. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Should I drink something cold to wake my baby?

No. This is a myth. If movements are reduced, call your midwife.

Do babies move less near the end?

No. Babies keep moving up to and during labour.
