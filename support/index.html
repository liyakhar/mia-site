Support

Last updated 23 October 2026. Here is how to get help with Mia.

Something urgent?

Mia is not an emergency service. If you are worried about yourself or your baby, call your maternity unit or your local emergency number now.

Contact us

Email liyakharitonova@gmail.com. Please do not send urgent medical information by email.

Signing in

You can sign in with Apple, Google or email to back up your data. If you forgot your email password, tap “Forgot password?” and Mia emails you a code from hello@miapregnancy.com.

Plans

Plans are managed by Apple. To cancel Monthly or Yearly, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, tap Restore purchase on Mia’s plan screen.

Delete your data

In Mia, open Profile, then Privacy and data. Delete All Data removes everything Mia keeps on your iPhone. Delete account removes your account and backup from our server.
