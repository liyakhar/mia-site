Hospital bag checklist: what to pack for labour and after

A packed bag takes one worry away. This is a short list, so you bring only what you need.

Summary
  • For labourYour birth plan and notes, comfortable clothes, snacks and drinks, a lip balm and a phone charger.
  • For afterLoose clothes, comfy underwear, maternity pads, toiletries and a nursing bra if you plan to breastfeed.
  • For babyA few sleepsuits, vests, a hat, nappies and a car seat if you are driving home.
  • When to packPack a few weeks early, and by 37 weeks at the latest.
01

When to pack

Pack a bag even if you plan a home birth, in case you need to go in. Have everything ready by 37 weeks at the latest.1

You might give birth on your due date, but do not bet on it. Pack for at least a couple of days.1

02

For labour

Your antenatal notes. Keep them with you at all times in the last months.1

One or two comfy nighties or big t-shirts. Labour rooms are warm. A comfy change of clothes.1

Drinks and snacks for you and your birth partner, something to read or listen to, a face cloth and a water spray, lip balm, hair bobbles, and a pillow from home.1

Phone, headphones and chargers, bank cards and change for the car park, toiletries, your medications, and a TENS machine if you want one.1

03

For after the birth

Front-opening nighties or loose shirts, a light dressing gown and slippers, and a couple of packets of maternity pads. Do not use tampons.1

5 or 6 pairs of knickers, 2 or 3 nursing or comfy bras, breast pads, and nipple cream. Clothes to come home in that are comfy and loose.1

If you are bottle-feeding, sterile bottles, teats and formula.1

04

For your baby

Up to 5 sleepsuits and vests, a pack of newborn nappies, cotton wool and water for cleaning, and a baby blanket or shawl. Avoid wipes for the first few weeks.1

Clothes to come home in, such as a hat, scratch mittens, socks and a pram suit if it is cold.1

You will also need a car seat to bring your baby home.1

05

Tips

You may prefer two bags, one for labour and one for after. Put them in a wheeled suitcase.1

Pack a nappy, hat and vest at the top so your birth partner can find them fast.1

Your hospital can usually provide basics like towels and blankets. Ask your midwife for a list specific to your unit.1

Good to know
When should I pack my bag?

A few weeks early, and by 37 weeks at the latest.

Do I need a bag for a home birth?

Yes. Have one ready in case you need to go in.
