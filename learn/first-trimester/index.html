First trimester: what to expect

The first trimester can feel big and quiet at the same time. Mia explains what is common, what helps, and when to call someone.

Common symptoms

Tiredness, nausea, tender breasts and needing to pee more are very common.

Nausea

Small, regular snacks and sips of water can help. If you cannot keep anything down, call your midwife or doctor.

Your first scan

The dating scan is usually offered around weeks 10 to 14.

Call if

You have heavy bleeding, strong one-sided pain or feel very unwell. Get help the same day.

Based on NHS guidance on the first trimester and NICE antenatal care guidance. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

What should I take in early pregnancy?

In the UK, 400 micrograms of folic acid daily is advised before pregnancy and until week 12. Ask your midwife what suits you.

Is spotting normal?

Light spotting can be common but always tell your midwife. Call at once if bleeding is heavy or with pain.
