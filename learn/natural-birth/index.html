Natural birth, explained honestly

A natural birth means labour that starts and moves on its own, with as little medical help as is safe for you. Mia explains what helps, how strong the proof is, and every other option too.

Move and stay upright

Walking, swaying and kneeling in early labour can help labour move along. Research links upright positions with a shorter first stage.

Continuous support

Having one person with you the whole time, like a doula or partner, is linked with fewer interventions and a better experience.

Dim light and calm

A quiet, private room helps you feel safe. Feeling safe helps labour hormones do their work.

Every option is fine

Pain relief, induction or a caesarean can be the right choice. Mia explains them too, without judgement.

Based on Cochrane reviews of upright positions in labour and of continuous support in labour. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Is natural birth safer?

It depends on your health and your pregnancy. Talk it through with your midwife.

Can I change my mind in labour?

Yes. Your birth plan is a wish list, not a contract.
