Good to know.

Can I try Plus for free?

Yes. Mia Plus comes with 7 days free. You can cancel before the trial ends and pay nothing.

What is Whole pregnancy?

One payment of €59.99 for 10 months of Mia Plus, enough for a whole pregnancy. It does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in your Apple Account settings on your iPhone. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens when I reach my daily messages?

Mia tells you, and your messages refill the next day. Your week, trackers and notes keep working.

Is Mia a doctor?

No. Mia teaches and supports. She tells you when to call your midwife.
