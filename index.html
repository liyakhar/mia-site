Questions

Good to know.

Is Mia a doctor?

No. Mia gives general information from trusted sources like the NHS. She does not diagnose, and she tells you when to call your midwife or maternity unit.

Where do the answers come from?

Health answers are based on official guidance, such as the NHS, and every answer shows its source.

Is my data private?

Yes. What you write stays yours. We never sell your data or show you ads.

What does it cost?

Mia is free to start. Mia Plus gives you more time with Mia. See the pricing page for details.
