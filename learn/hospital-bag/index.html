What to pack in your hospital bag

A packed bag takes one worry away. Mia gives you a short, calm list so you only bring what you need.

Start free
01

For labour

Your birth plan and notes, comfortable clothes, snacks and drinks, a lip balm and a phone charger.

02

For after

Loose clothes, comfy underwear, maternity pads, toiletries and a nursing bra if you plan to breastfeed.

03

For baby

A few sleepsuits, vests, a hat, nappies and a car seat if you are driving home.

04

When to pack

Many people pack by week 36. Keep the bag by the door.

Based on NHS suggestions for what to pack for labour and birth. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Do I need to bring my own pillow?

Many people like to. Check your unit’s advice.

What should my birth partner bring?

Snacks, a charger, cash for parking and a change of clothes.
