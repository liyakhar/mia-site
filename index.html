Questions

Good to know

Is Mia a doctor?

No. Mia teaches, she doesn’t diagnose. She will always tell you when to call your midwife or doctor.

Where does the knowledge come from?

NHS, NICE and WHO guidance, Cochrane reviews, royal college advice and midwifery research. Every lesson links its sources.

Is it only about natural birth?

Natural birth is where Mia goes deepest, but she also covers pain relief, induction and caesarean birth, plainly and without judgement.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your records stay on your iPhone, and there are no ads, ever.

What does it cost?

Free to start. Mia Plus unlocks every course from €8.99 a month, with 7 days free.
