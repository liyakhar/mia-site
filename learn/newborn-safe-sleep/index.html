Safe sleep for newborns

Safe sleep is simple once you know it. Mia gives you the short list so you can rest more easily.

Start free
01

On the back

Always put baby to sleep on their back, for every sleep.

02

Own space

A firm, flat mattress in a cot or Moses basket in your room for the first six months.

03

Keep it clear

No pillows, bumpers, loose blankets or soft toys in the sleep space.

04

Avoid risk

Do not sleep with baby on a sofa or armchair, and do not smoke around baby.

Based on NHS and The Lullaby Trust guidance on safer sleep. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Is it safe to share a bed?

The safest place is a separate cot in your room. Read the risks, especially if you smoke, drink or take sedating medicine.

How warm should the room be?

Between 16 and 20 degrees is advised. Dress baby lightly.
