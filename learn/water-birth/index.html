Labour and birth in water

Warm water can help you relax and move freely in labour, and many birth centres and hospitals have a pool. Here is what the research found, and how to ask for one.

Start free
01

Why water helps

Warm water supports your weight and can ease pain. Some people need less other pain relief.

02

What the research found

The large UK POOL study looked at tens of thousands of births. For women with low-risk pregnancies, birth in water was not linked with more harm to babies.

03

Who it suits

Usually people with a healthy, low-risk pregnancy. Your midwife will tell you if it suits you.

04

Plan ahead

Ask early if your birth place has a pool, and what happens if it is in use.

Based on the POOL cohort study (UK) and NHS guidance on water birth. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Can I have pain relief in the pool?

Gas and air, yes. For an epidural you leave the pool.

Do I have to give birth in the water?

No. Many people labour in the pool and give birth outside it.
