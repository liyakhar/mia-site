Postnatal depression: signs and where to get help

Many new parents feel low. Mia explains the difference between baby blues and depression and how to get help fast.

Start free
01

Baby blues

Tearfulness and mood swings in the first two weeks are common and pass.

02

Postnatal depression

If low mood, anxiety or feeling unable to cope lasts more than two weeks, speak to your midwife, health visitor or GP.

03

Partners too

Partners can also feel low. Help is for them as well.

04

Urgent help

If you have thoughts of harming yourself or your baby, call your GP, 111 or 999 now.

Based on NHS and NICE guidance on perinatal mental health. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Is it my fault?

No. It is an illness, not a failure, and it is treatable.

Will treatment affect breastfeeding?

Some treatments are fine while breastfeeding. Your doctor can advise.
