Best positions for labour and birth

How you move in labour can make a real difference. Mia shows simple positions to try and explains why they help.

Stay upright

Standing, walking and leaning on a partner use gravity and can help baby move down.

Use a birth ball

Sitting and rocking on a ball can ease backache and keep your hips loose.

Hands and knees

This takes weight off your back and can help if baby is lying back to back.

Change often

Try a new position every 30 minutes or so. Your body will tell you what feels right.

Based on the Cochrane review of positions in the first stage of labour and NICE intrapartum care guidance. Mia teaches. Your midwife knows you best.

Good to know

Can I move with a monitor or drip?

Often, yes. Ask your midwife which positions work with your equipment.

Is lying on my back bad?

It is not unsafe for everyone, but upright and side positions are linked with a shorter first stage.
