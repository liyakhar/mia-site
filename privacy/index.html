Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. This policy covers the Mia: Pregnancy app for iPhone and the website miapregnancy.com.

Who is responsible

The controller is Alexandre de Pape, an individual seller, Avenue Alexandre Dumas 6, 1410 Waterloo, Belgium.

For any privacy question or request, email liyakharitonova@gmail.com. Mia is for adults aged 18 or older.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile, pregnancy week, tracking (feelings, symptoms, weight, baby movements, contractions), visits, notes, saved items and chat history are stored on your iPhone. They are not synchronised through iCloud and are not used for advertising. Mia has no ads and no cross-app tracking.

Accounts

An account is optional. You need one only to back up your data and bring it back on a new iPhone. You can sign in three ways:

  • Sign in with Apple: Mia receives a code from Apple and keeps only a one-way code made from your Apple user ID. Mia does not receive your name, and does not keep your email.
  • Sign in with Google: Mia receives a code from Google and keeps only a one-way code made from your Google account ID. Mia does not keep your name, email or photo.
  • Email and password: Mia keeps your email address and a scrambled form of your password (a salted hash; Mia never stores the password itself). Your email is used only to sign you in and to send you a code if you forget your password.

Password reset codes are sent through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which delivers the email on our behalf. A code is valid for 15 minutes.

We use this information to provide the account you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). It is kept until you delete your account in the app (Profile, Privacy and data) or ask us to delete it.

Backup

When you are signed in, Mia packs your records, notes, chats and memory notes on your iPhone and encrypts them there with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain. Only the encrypted file reaches Mia’s server. We cannot read it. Deleting your account removes the backup from our server straight away.

Optional chat with Mia

Chat is optional. Before Mia first answers, the app asks whether Mia may use your app information. If you say yes, each question sends your message, recent chat history and your app information (first name, pregnancy week, recent tracking, upcoming visits, recent notes and saved items) to Mia’s service, which passes it to OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to (currently Anthropic’s Claude) for a reply. If you choose “Only use my messages”, only your message and recent chat history are sent. You can change or stop this at any time in the app.

Mia asks OpenRouter for a zero-data-retention route. Unless you switch on “Save my chats”, Mia’s server does not store your messages or Mia’s replies, and never writes chat text to its logs.

If you switch on “Save my chats”, the server keeps your messages and replies with a one-way code made from your iPhone’s App Attest key, to improve Mia’s answers and safety, based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). They are deleted after 12 months, or straight away when you switch the setting off.

Listen and saved videos

If you tap Listen, the reply text is sent through Mia’s service and OpenRouter to OpenAI’s audio model to create speech, which is cached on your iPhone. Mia’s service stores neither the text nor the audio.

If you save a link to a public Instagram, TikTok or YouTube video, Mia’s service downloads its audio, has it written down and summarised the same way, returns the result to your iPhone and deletes the audio straight away.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. Mia receives Apple’s signed purchase record to check which plan you have and how many messages you have left. Mia does not receive your payment details. Usage counters reset each day and month.

Security and technical records

Mia uses encrypted connections and Apple App Attest to protect its service. Short-lived technical records (usage counters and anti-abuse records) contain no chat text, notes or tracking content.

Who helps us run Mia

  • Railway (servers, United States)
  • OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to (chat, Listen and saved videos)
  • Brevo (password reset emails, France)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, purchases, App Attest)
  • Google (Sign in with Google)

Some of these process data outside the European Economic Area. Their own privacy terms and transfer safeguards, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, apply to that processing.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, object to or limit its use, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use. Most of this you can do in the app: Delete All Data removes everything on your iPhone, and Profile, Privacy and data lets you delete your account and backup.

For anything else, email liyakharitonova@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (autoriteprotectiondonnees.be) or the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update it here and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again in the app.
